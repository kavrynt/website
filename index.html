What is Kavrynt?

Kavrynt is an open-source AI infrastructure platform for deploying, securing and operating AI workloads on Kubernetes.

Why are we building it?

AI infrastructure today lacks standardized lifecycle management, governance, GitOps workflows and operational tooling. Kavrynt aims to provide a Kubernetes-inspired control plane for AI infrastructure.
